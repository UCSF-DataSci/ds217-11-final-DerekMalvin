KEY PATTERNS IDENTIFIED
=======================

TEMPORAL TRENDS:
- Air Temperature shows a clear annual seasonal cycle with higher values during warmer months and lower values during colder months.
- No long-term trends can be established from the monthly averages alone.
- Wind variables all seem to have a very strong correlation with one another.

SEASONAL PATTERNS:
- The monthly averages show that there is a seasonal pattern evident, peaking in the summer months and going to the lowest points in winter months.
- Temperatures are highest in summer months (around July–August).
- Temperatures are lowest in winter months (January–February & December).

CORRELATIONS:
- Rain-related variables are showing a weak correlation with one another.
- Wind-related variables are all showing a positve correlation with one another
- There is a strong correlation between Air Temperature and Wet Bulb Temperature.
